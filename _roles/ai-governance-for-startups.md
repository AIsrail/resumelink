## What does a Startup AI Governance Lead do?

Startup AI Governance Leads set up lightweight, practical AI governance, covering policies, risk reviews and documentation, so a startup can sell to enterprises and comply with laws like the EU AI Act.

## A typical week

- Reviewing new AI features for risk
- Answering customer security and AI questionnaires
- Updating the AI inventory and documentation
- Training teams on the AI use policy

## How to show this role on your resume

Tie the program to business outcomes and name frameworks. Recruiters search for EU AI Act, ISO 42001 and NIST AI RMF.
