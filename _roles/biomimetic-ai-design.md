## What does a Biomimetic AI Designer do?

Biomimetic AI Designers borrow principles from biology, such as swarms, neural systems and evolution, to design AI systems and products that are more efficient, adaptive or robust.

## A typical week

- Reviewing biological literature for design ideas
- Building simulations
- Testing algorithms against baselines
- Presenting findings to engineering teams
