## What does an AI Event Planner do?

AI Event Planners use AI tools for venue sourcing, attendee matching, logistics and personalization, freeing time for the human work of hospitality and live problem-solving.

## A typical week

- Comparing venue and vendor proposals
- Building run-of-show documents
- Configuring event app and matchmaking
- Walking through contingency plans with the team

## How to show this role on your resume

Lead with scale (attendees, budget, sessions) and outcomes (satisfaction, meetings, cost). List the event platforms you run.
