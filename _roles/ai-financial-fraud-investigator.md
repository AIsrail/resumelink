## What does an AI Financial Fraud Investigator do?

AI Financial Fraud Investigators work the cases that automated detection flags: they confirm fraud, trace funds, document evidence and feed lessons back into the models.

## A typical week

- Working the alert queue and escalated cases
- Tracing funds across accounts and wallets
- Writing case narratives and regulatory reports
- Meeting with model teams on false positives

## How to show this role on your resume

Quantify case volume, recoveries and quality scores. Name the typologies and systems you know.
