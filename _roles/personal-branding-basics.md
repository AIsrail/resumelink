## What is this guide about?

A practical guide to building a clear, credible personal brand, including headline, story, proof and online presence, so recruiters understand your value in seconds.

## Steps to follow

- Updating profile headline and summary
- Sharing one piece of work or insight
- Reaching out to a few people in your field
- Collecting proof: metrics, testimonials, samples

## How it shows up on your resume

Keep your resume, LinkedIn and portfolio consistent in title, specialty and key achievements.
