## What does an AI Real Estate Investment Analyst do?

AI Real Estate Investment Analysts underwrite property deals using financial models, market data and machine learning, helping investors decide what to buy, hold or sell.

## A typical week

- Screening new deals
- Building and stress-testing models
- Touring assets and meeting brokers
- Presenting to investment committee

## How to show this role on your resume

Quantify deal volume, closed value and model improvements. Name modeling tools.
