## What does a Human-in-the-Loop Workflow Designer do?

Human-in-the-Loop Workflow Designers decide where people review, correct or approve AI outputs, and design those steps so humans stay effective instead of rubber-stamping.

## A typical week

- Analyzing review queues and error rates
- Refining guidelines with reviewers
- Improving review tooling
- Reporting quality to stakeholders

## How to show this role on your resume

Quantify quality, speed and review share. Mention tools and methods.
