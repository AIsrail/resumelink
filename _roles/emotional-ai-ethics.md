## What does an Emotional AI Ethics Specialist do?

Emotional AI Ethics Specialists review systems that detect, simulate or respond to human emotions, such as companions, voice assistants and sentiment tools, to prevent manipulation, harm and misuse.

## A typical week

- Reviewing product designs and prompts
- Running user studies on wellbeing
- Advising on safeguards
- Tracking regulation

Show decisions influenced and risks prevented. Mention frameworks and regulations.
