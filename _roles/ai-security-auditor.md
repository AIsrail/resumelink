## What does an AI Security Auditor do?

AI Security Auditors assess whether AI systems and their controls meet security standards and regulations, producing independent evidence for boards, customers and regulators.

## A typical week

- Planning audits and requesting evidence
- Testing controls and interviewing owners
- Writing findings and recommendations
- Following up on remediation

## How to show this role on your resume

Quantify audits, systems and findings. Name standards and certifications.
