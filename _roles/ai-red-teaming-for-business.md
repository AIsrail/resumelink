## What does a Business AI Red Team Lead do?

Business AI Red Team Leads organize structured attacks on a company's AI products and internal assistants, covering security, safety, bias and misuse, so risks are found before customers or attackers find them.

## A typical week

- Scoping exercises for new features
- Coordinating testers and tools
- Triaging findings with product teams
- Reporting risk trends to leadership

## How to show this role on your resume

Describe program scope and outcomes (issues found, fixed, prevented). Name frameworks used.
