## What does an AI Smart City Coordinator do?

AI Smart City Coordinators connect city departments, vendors and residents to deploy data and AI projects (traffic, lighting, waste, safety) that improve services while protecting privacy.

## A typical week

- Coordinating departments and vendors
- Tracking pilot KPIs
- Preparing procurement documents
- Engaging residents and council members

## How to show this role on your resume

Describe pilots, budgets and measured service improvements. Mention governance work.
