## What does an AI Project Manager do?

AI Project Managers deliver AI and machine learning projects from idea to production, managing data dependencies, model uncertainty and stakeholder expectations.

## A typical week

- Running stand-ups and sprint reviews
- Tracking data and model milestones
- Managing risks and dependencies
- Updating stakeholders on results

## How to show this role on your resume

Quantify projects, budgets and outcomes. Mention both PM methods and ML lifecycle familiarity.
