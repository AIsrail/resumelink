## What does an AI Creative Process Lead do?

AI Creative Process Leads build repeatable ways for creative teams to use generative AI for ideation and production, without losing brand voice, originality or legal safety.

## A typical week

- Running ideation sessions that mix AI exploration with team critique
- Maintaining prompt and style libraries per client
- Reviewing outputs for brand fit and rights issues
- Coaching team members on new tools

## How to show this role on your resume

Describe the system you built (workflow, policy, library) and its effect on throughput or quality. Name tools, but lead with outcomes.
