## What does an AI-Assisted Independent Journalist do?

AI-assisted independent journalists use AI for research, transcription, data analysis and translation, while keeping full responsibility for verification, sourcing and editorial judgment.

## A typical week

- Researching leads with AI-assisted document search
- Interviewing sources and verifying claims
- Analyzing datasets and building charts
- Publishing and engaging with readers

## How to show this role on your resume

List published investigations with impact (citations, policy change). Describe your verification workflow briefly.

The resume bullets on this page are illustrative examples. Replace the numbers with your own verified results.
