## What does an AI Retail Strategist do?

AI Retail Strategists decide where AI creates value in retail (pricing, assortment, personalization, supply) and build the business case and roadmap to capture it.

## A typical week

- Reviewing pilot results
- Building business cases
- Aligning with merchandising and operations
- Presenting roadmaps to leadership

## How to show this role on your resume

Quantify value identified and captured. Mention pilots with controls.

The resume bullets on this page are illustrative examples. Replace the numbers with your own verified results.
