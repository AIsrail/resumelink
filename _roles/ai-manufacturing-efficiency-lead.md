## What does an AI Manufacturing Efficiency Lead do?

AI Manufacturing Efficiency Leads raise output and quality on the shop floor by combining lean methods with AI for predictive maintenance, quality inspection and scheduling.

## A typical week

- Walking the lines and reviewing OEE losses
- Prioritizing maintenance alerts with engineering
- Running improvement workshops
- Reporting results to plant leadership

## How to show this role on your resume

Quote OEE, scrap and downtime changes with baselines. Mention lean certifications.
