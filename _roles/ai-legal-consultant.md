## What does an AI Legal Consultant do?

AI Legal Consultants advise organizations on the legal side of AI (contracts, liability, IP, privacy and regulation) and help legal teams adopt AI tools responsibly.

## A typical week

- Reviewing AI vendor and customer contracts
- Advising on product regulatory questions
- Tracking new laws and guidance
- Training lawyers on AI tool use

## How to show this role on your resume

Show matters handled and business impact. List jurisdictions and regulations you advise on.
