## What does an AI Environmental Analyst do?

AI Environmental Analysts use satellite imagery, sensor data and machine learning to monitor ecosystems, emissions and land use, turning data into decisions for companies, NGOs and governments.

## A typical week

- Processing new satellite scenes and validating model alerts
- Ground-truthing results with field partners
- Building dashboards for sustainability teams
- Writing methodology notes for reports

## How to show this role on your resume

Quantify the area, frequency and decisions your monitoring supported. Mention data sources by name; reviewers check for real remote-sensing experience.
