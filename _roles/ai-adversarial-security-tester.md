## What does an AI Adversarial Security Tester do?

AI Adversarial Security Testers attack machine learning systems the way real adversaries would: prompt injection, data poisoning, model evasion and data extraction, then help teams fix what they find.

## A typical week

- Scoping tests for a new AI feature with product and security teams
- Running automated and manual jailbreak and injection campaigns
- Writing reproducible findings with severity and fix guidance
- Retesting fixes and updating the regression suite

## How to show this role on your resume

Quantify findings (number, severity, time to fix) and name the frameworks you test against. Link to public write-ups or CVEs if you have them.

The resume bullets on this page are illustrative examples. Replace the numbers with your own verified results.
