## What does a Neurodiversity Inclusion Specialist do?

Neurodiversity Inclusion Specialists help employers recruit, support and retain neurodivergent people, and make sure AI tools in hiring and work are accessible and fair to them.

## A typical week

- Supporting candidates and employees
- Advising managers
- Reviewing tools and processes for accessibility
- Running training

## How to show this role on your resume

Quantify people supported and retention. Mention accessibility credentials.
