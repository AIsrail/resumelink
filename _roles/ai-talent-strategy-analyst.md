## What does an AI Talent Strategy Analyst do?

AI Talent Strategy Analysts use workforce data and AI to forecast skill needs, find internal talent and shape hiring and reskilling plans.

## A typical week

- Analyzing workforce data
- Updating skill forecasts
- Supporting leaders with planning
- Reviewing fairness of recommendations

## How to show this role on your resume

Quantify people covered and decisions supported. Mention privacy and fairness safeguards.
