## What does an AI Strategic Foresight Analyst do?

AI Strategic Foresight Analysts combine scenario planning with AI-assisted research to map possible futures and help leadership make robust long-term decisions.

## A typical week

- Reviewing new signals surfaced by AI research tools
- Updating scenario narratives and indicators
- Preparing leadership workshop materials
- Briefing teams on implications for their plans

## How to show this role on your resume

Name the decisions your scenarios informed and the scale involved. Mention methods (e.g. 2x2 scenarios, three horizons) to signal rigor.
