## What does an AI-First Organizational Designer do?

AI-First Organizational Designers reshape roles, teams and decision rights so that people and AI systems work together effectively, and so the change sticks.

## A typical week

- Mapping current tasks and where AI changes them
- Workshops with managers on new role designs
- Planning reskilling paths with L&D
- Tracking adoption and sentiment metrics

## How to show this role on your resume

Quote scope (headcount, functions) and outcomes (adoption, cycle time, retention). Mention the change framework you used.
